02 / About

A short version.

Hi, I'm Henry. I'm a software engineer based in London, currently working at a small startup where I'm building the mobile app for an AI receptionist agent. Before that I studied Computer Science at Exeter.

I've always been interested in finance as much as in software. In 2025 I split an internship between investment work and engineering at a London advisory firm — sitting in pitch meetings during the week, then building tools to automate parts of the workflow.

The stuff I most enjoy building tends to live somewhere between the two. My dissertation was a reinforcement learning trading agent. Market Brief surfaces equity metrics for retail investors. I'm not picky about which side of the line a job sits on — software, finance, or anywhere they meet.

Outside of work: rugby for a local club, chess, golf, and a guitar I'm getting marginally less bad at.
